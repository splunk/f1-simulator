Prepare F1 25 for your next Data Drivers event. Work through these settings in order, then run a complete test race before guests arrive.

1. [Start a Grand Prix](/event_guide/grand-prix/) — choose your track, team and driver.
2. [Set the assists](/event_guide/assists/) — make the simulator approachable for participants.
3. [Configure rules and flags](/event_guide/rules/).
4. [Set the weekend structure](/event_guide/structure/) — go straight to a short race.
5. [Configure simulation settings](/event_guide/simulation-settings/).
6. [Set the weather and time of day](/event_guide/weather/).
7. [Go racing](/event_guide/go-racing/) — start the event and prepare for the next driver.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
**Connect telemetry before the first race**

Follow [Game Telemetry Setup](/f1-2025/telemetry/) to point each rig at the collector. Use [Running an Event](/f1-2025/managing-collectors/) for driver names, recording and session completion.
{{< /callout >}}
