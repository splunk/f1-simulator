1. Select **START EVENT**. Press **X** to skip the introductory scenes until the car is stopped on the track in cockpit view.

   ![Car ready to start the race](../images/start_race.webp)

2. On the collector, enter the attendee’s name on the correct rig and select **Ready**.
3. In the game, press **X** to select **Start Race**.
4. After the race, continue to the results screen. Leave the game there until the collector saves the result and the rig card opens for the next driver.
5. In the game, select **Advance** to start the next Grand Prix. Skip the introductory scenes until the car is back on the track.
6. Enter the next attendee’s name and select **Ready** before starting their race.

See [Running an Event](/f1-2025/managing-collectors/) if a result does not appear or a race needs to be reset.
